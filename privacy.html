Privacy

pokerDiary stores the data you enter about your home games (players, buy-ins, cash-outs, results) so the members of your group can see and settle them. Sign-in uses Google or Apple; we store your name, email and a profile picture URL to identify you to your group.

Data is stored with Supabase (EU, Frankfurt). We do not sell data or show ads. To delete your account and data, email the address in the app store listing.
